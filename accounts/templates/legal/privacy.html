Privacy Policy

Effective 27 July 2026

This policy explains how Yalla Market collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you use our mobile application and related services.

Information we collect

  • Account information, including your name, username, email address, phone number, password hash, profile photo, gender, and birth date when provided.
  • Delivery information, including saved addresses, recipient details, and location coordinates you choose to provide.
  • Order and service records, such as products, prices, delivery status, support interactions, and partner applications.
  • Device and technical information needed for authentication, security, diagnostics, and push notifications.

How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, process and deliver orders, send essential updates, provide support, prevent fraud, maintain service reliability, meet legal obligations, and improve Yalla Market. We do not sell personal information.

When information is shared

We share only what is necessary with participating markets, delivery representatives, infrastructure and notification providers, professional advisers, and authorities where legally required. Providers must handle information for the services they perform for us.

Retention

Account and saved-delivery data is kept while your account is active. After account deletion, direct identifiers and saved addresses are deleted or anonymized. Limited transaction records may be retained in anonymized form where needed for accounting, disputes, fraud prevention, or legal obligations. Backup copies expire through normal retention cycles.

Your choices and rights

You can review or update profile details in the app and control notification permission in device settings. You may delete your account from Account → Delete Account. You may also use our web deletion instructions. Depending on applicable law, you may ask to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your personal information.

Security and children

We use access controls, encrypted transport, password hashing, session revocation, and operational safeguards. No system is completely secure. Yalla Market is not intended for children who cannot legally consent to online services in their jurisdiction.
