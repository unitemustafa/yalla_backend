Request deletion on WhatsApp

What deletion does

Your profile identifiers, authentication credentials, saved addresses, device tokens, notifications, verification codes, and favourites are deleted or anonymized, and all sessions are revoked. The action cannot be undone.

What may be retained

Active orders must be completed or cancelled first. Limited order and transaction records may remain in anonymized form for accounting, dispute handling, fraud prevention, and legal compliance. They are no longer available through the deleted account. Backup copies expire through normal retention cycles.
